How to Measure Height

Standing height without shoes, measured against a wall or stadiometer.

Monthly Standing wall

Steps

  1. Stand barefoot with heels, upper back, and head near a flat wall.
  2. Look straight ahead and keep the measuring edge level.
  3. Record the value in centimeters after a normal inhale.

Common Mistakes

  • Measuring with shoes.
  • Tilting the head up.
  • Using a soft floor.

Tracking Note

Height is mostly a stable frame measurement for adult tracking. Use it to normalize ratios rather than treating small day-to-day differences as meaningful change.
