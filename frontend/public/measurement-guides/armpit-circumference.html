How to Measure Armpit Circumference

Upper chest circumference near the armpit line.

Weekly Upper chest tape

Steps

  1. Wrap tape high around the chest, close to the armpits.
  2. Stand relaxed with arms down.
  3. Record after a normal exhale.

Common Mistakes

  • Lat flare.
  • Deep inhale.

Tracking Note

Upper chest measurements are sensitive to breathing and arm position; standardize both.
