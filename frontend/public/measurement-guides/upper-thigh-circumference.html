How to Measure Upper Thigh Circumference

Upper thigh circumference near the glute fold.

Weekly Thigh tape

Steps

  1. Stand relaxed with weight evenly distributed.
  2. Measure the upper thigh near the glute fold.
  3. Use the same side and same distance from the hip each time.

Common Mistakes

  • Measuring lower each week.
  • Flexing the quad.

Tracking Note

Upper thigh measurements need the same side and landmark to avoid false trend movement.
