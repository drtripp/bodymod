How to Measure Bicep Circumference

Upper-arm circumference at the largest point.

Weekly Arm tape

Steps

  1. Let the arm hang relaxed unless deliberately tracking flexed size.
  2. Measure around the largest upper-arm point.
  3. Use the same arm and state each time.

Common Mistakes

  • Mixing flexed and relaxed.
  • Tape slipping into the armpit.

Tracking Note

Choose relaxed or flexed tracking deliberately, then keep that state consistent.
