How to Measure Hip Circumference

Largest circumference around hips and buttocks.

Weekly Hip tape

Steps

  1. Stand with feet close and weight even.
  2. Wrap tape around the largest hip and buttock point.
  3. Check in a mirror that the tape is level.

Common Mistakes

  • Tape tilted upward.
  • Feet too wide.

Tracking Note

Hip circumference changes with tape height and stance. Keep feet and landmark consistent.
