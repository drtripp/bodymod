How to Measure Ankle Circumference

Ankle circumference at the narrowest point above the ankle bones.

Monthly Ankle tape

Steps

  1. Stand or sit with the foot relaxed and flat.
  2. Wrap tape around the narrowest point just above the ankle bones.
  3. Keep the tape snug without pressing into skin.

Common Mistakes

  • Measuring over socks.
  • Placing tape too high on the calf.

Tracking Note

Ankle circumference is mostly a frame reference. Expect slow movement and prioritize repeatability.
