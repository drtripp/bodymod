How to Measure Head Circumference

Tape around the largest head circumference.

Monthly Head tape

Steps

  1. Place the tape above the eyebrows and around the widest back of the skull.
  2. Keep the tape snug without compressing hair or skin.
  3. Measure twice and use the repeatable value.

Common Mistakes

  • Tape too low at the forehead.
  • Compressing hair.

Tracking Note

Head circumference changes slowly for adult tracking. Use the same tape path each time.
