How to Measure Underbust Circumference

Circumference directly under the chest.

Weekly Underbust tape

Steps

  1. Place tape directly under the chest or pec line.
  2. Keep it snug but not compressive.
  3. Measure after a relaxed exhale.

Common Mistakes

  • Letting tape sit on soft chest tissue.
  • Pulling tight.

Tracking Note

Underbust is most useful when the tape sits at the same under-chest landmark each week.
