How to Measure Upper Forearm Circumference

Forearm circumference near the largest upper forearm point.

Weekly Forearm tape

Steps

  1. Relax hand and forearm.
  2. Measure around the largest forearm point below the elbow.
  3. Keep the tape perpendicular to the forearm.

Common Mistakes

  • Gripping hard.
  • Measuring too close to elbow.

Tracking Note

Forearm size can change with grip tension during measurement, so keep the hand relaxed.
