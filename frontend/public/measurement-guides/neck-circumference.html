How to Measure Neck Circumference

Tape around the lower neck without compressing tissue.

Weekly Neck tape

Steps

  1. Stand tall with shoulders relaxed.
  2. Wrap tape around the lower neck near the Adam's apple level.
  3. Keep the tape level and record a relaxed value.

Common Mistakes

  • Pulling the tape tight.
  • Shrugging.

Tracking Note

Neck circumference feeds formula estimates, so repeat the same relaxed posture and tape tension.
