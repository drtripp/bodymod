How to Measure Mid-Thigh Circumference

Thigh circumference halfway between hip and knee.

Weekly Thigh tape

Steps

  1. Mark halfway between hip crease and top of kneecap.
  2. Wrap tape perpendicular to the leg.
  3. Record relaxed, not flexed.

Common Mistakes

  • Inconsistent height.
  • Tape angled down.

Tracking Note

Marking the midpoint helps keep mid-thigh logs comparable across weeks.
