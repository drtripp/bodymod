How to Measure Wrist Circumference

Wrist circumference at the narrowest point.

Monthly Wrist tape

Steps

  1. Wrap tape around the narrowest wrist point.
  2. Keep hand relaxed and tape snug.
  3. Record as a frame reference, not a weekly change target.

Common Mistakes

  • Measuring over watch marks.
  • Pulling the tape tight.

Tracking Note

Wrist circumference is a frame-reference field. Consistent tape tension matters more than frequent checks.
