How to Measure Calf Circumference

Largest calf circumference.

Weekly Calf tape

Steps

  1. Stand with weight evenly distributed.
  2. Find the largest point of the calf.
  3. Measure the same leg each time unless tracking left/right later.

Common Mistakes

  • Measuring seated.
  • Tape not level.

Tracking Note

Standing calf measurements are more comparable than seated values when tracking body changes.
