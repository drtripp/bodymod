How to Measure Pant Waist Circumference

Circumference where pants normally sit.

Weekly Pant waist

Steps

  1. Place tape at the natural pants waistband position.
  2. Keep posture relaxed and tape level.
  3. Record separately from narrowest waist.

Common Mistakes

  • Confusing with narrowest waist.
  • Measuring over bulky clothing.

Tracking Note

Pant waist helps fit estimates. Keep it separate from the narrowest-waist measurement.
