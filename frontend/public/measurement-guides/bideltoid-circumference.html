How to Measure Bideltoid Circumference

Tape around the shoulders at the widest deltoid line.

Weekly Shoulder loop

Steps

  1. Wrap tape around the shoulders and upper chest at the deltoid peak.
  2. Keep arms down and relaxed.
  3. Keep tape level around the back.

Common Mistakes

  • Measuring chest only.
  • Raising the arms.
  • Letting the tape slope around the back.

Tracking Note

Bideltoid circumference is used in shoulder-mass and shoulder-to-waist context. Keep arm position boring and repeatable so training changes are not mixed with measurement posture.
