How to Measure Waist Circumference

Narrowest relaxed torso circumference.

Weekly Waist tape

Steps

  1. Find the narrowest relaxed point between ribs and hips.
  2. Keep tape level and do not suck in.
  3. Measure after a normal exhale.

Common Mistakes

  • Measuring the pants waistband.
  • Bracing abs.
  • Pulling tape into skin.

Tracking Note

Waist circumference is one of the most sensitive body-composition fields in the app. Use the same landmark every week so trend movement is not confused with tape placement.
