How to Measure Nipple Circumference

Chest circumference across the nipple line.

Weekly Chest tape

Steps

  1. Wrap tape across the nipple line and around the back.
  2. Keep the tape level and parallel to the floor.
  3. Record a relaxed exhale value.

Common Mistakes

  • Over-inhaling.
  • Tape sloping upward on the back.

Tracking Note

Use the same chest line and breathing state so posture does not look like tissue change.
