How to Measure Weight

Scale weight under repeatable conditions.

Daily Scale

Steps

  1. Use the same scale on a hard surface.
  2. Weigh after waking and bathroom use when possible.
  3. Treat single-day changes as noise; use the trend.

Common Mistakes

  • Comparing different scales.
  • Reacting to sodium or meal weight.

Tracking Note

Weight is noisy. Log it consistently, then rely on smoothed trend movement and measurement context instead of a single reading.
