What it does

A body-change log built around measurements.

Bodymod is for people who want an honest, inspectable record of body-shape changes without turning the app into medical advice or a black-box coaching feed.

Measurements and silhouettes

Enter repeatable body measurements, inspect front and side SVG silhouettes, compare against targets, and save local snapshots.

Diet and training logs

Search dummy USDA-style foods, log meals and fluids, track workouts, and review progress without needing another app.

Protocols and reports

Attach protocols to goals, mark adherence, compare outcomes, and print progress reports for trainers or appointments.

Privacy stance

Useful before accounts exist.

Local by default

Current snapshots, diet logs, goals, protocols, and waitlist signups live in browser storage unless you export or share them.

Readable exports

JSON exports and encrypted local backups are part of the core product, not a paid escape hatch.

Clear limits

Placeholder data, formula estimates, and strategy corpus entries are labeled as informational and not medical advice.

Mobile and Pro

Native apps are planned after the web app hardens.

The current tier line is simple: all current tracking remains free. Future Pro is for compute, curation, sync, automation, and native integrations with marginal cost.
