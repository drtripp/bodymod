Terms Of Use Draft

Draft terms for review. This is a product-specific template, not a final legal agreement.

Prototype Status

Bodymod is a prototype for local-first body measurement tracking, comparison, diet logging, workouts, progress records, and informational strategy browsing. Current target profiles, reference percentiles, public guide copy, and corpus entries include scaffold or dummy data.

Acceptable Use

  • Use the app for personal logging, comparison, planning, and exported reports.
  • Do not use the app to harass, shame, profile, or make claims about another person without consent.
  • Do not treat scaffold target data or population charts as authoritative public claims.

User Responsibility

  • Users are responsible for the data they enter, export, back up, or share.
  • Share URLs may contain measurement values and should be handled as sensitive links.
  • Encrypted local backups depend on the passphrase the user chooses.

Informational Content

Strategy corpus entries, workout notes, diet targets, measurement guides, and methodology pages are informational. They are not individualized medical, nutritional, legal, or professional advice. High-risk corpus entries are explicitly separated from personalization.

Availability And Changes

The prototype can change as features, data sources, and policy decisions evolve. Local data availability depends on the user's browser storage and backups.

Draft review needed: entity name, jurisdiction, dispute terms, warranty limitations, account/payment terms, and mobile-app terms before public launch.
