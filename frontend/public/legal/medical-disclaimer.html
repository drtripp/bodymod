Medical Disclaimer Draft

Draft medical disclaimer for review. The current app is informational and does not diagnose, prescribe, or generate medical protocols.

Not Medical Advice

Bodymod is for tracking and informational comparison. It is not a substitute for a clinician, dietitian, mental-health professional, trainer, surgeon, pharmacist, or other qualified professional.

Measurements And Estimates

  • Body measurements, silhouettes, body-composition estimates, and percentiles are approximations.
  • Current percentiles are explicitly labeled as not NHANES-calibrated.
  • Population charts and gender-score charts are scaffold comparisons, not diagnoses or identity inference.

Diet, Workout, And Protocol Content

Calorie targets, macro targets, workouts, protocol notes, and trend estimates are planning aids. Users should consider health conditions, medications, injury status, and professional guidance before acting on training, diet, supplement, or procedure-related information.

High-Risk Topics

Surgical, pharmaceutical, clinical, and medical-adjacent strategy entries are informational only. They require extra acknowledgment in the app and are excluded from personalization. The app does not give dosing instructions, procedure instructions, sourcing guidance, or emergency advice.

Urgent Or Personal Concerns

Users with urgent symptoms, eating-disorder concerns, medication questions, injury, surgical questions, or mental-health concerns should contact qualified local professionals or emergency services as appropriate.
